# Barracks plan

Load `barracks-team` and `barracks-ba`. Verify the triage understanding against current source. Record the goal, scope, acceptance criteria, phases, ownership, checks, risks, supported technical decisions and unresolved product questions on the canonical story and in the session.

The implementation handoff explicitly directs the assigned Builder to load `barracks-team` and `barracks-builder`. Include the exact base SHA, configured target branch and protected refs. Keep the existing two Builder slots and independent review ownership explicit.

When the handoff is ready and current authorization covers implementation, request Factory's declared next phase with the live phase revision. If a product decision or required approval is missing, hand it to the Lead with the proposed next action. Factory's transition policy remains authoritative; a skill does not bypass it.
